\section{Introduction}
The field of robotics and automation in the industry has been rapidly developing. The first cobots were created in the 1990s but they were largely in research labs and automation programs. The breakthrough happened in 2008 when the UR5 was released from Universal robotics, making robots easier, cheaper and affordable to be ready deployed in the industry. \\

In today's industries there are many different companies producing cobots and grippers for various demands including Onrobot, SMC amongst other. Our project is loosely based upon the production and development of creating a gripper similarly to the industry purposes. Then subsequently interfacing it with the assigned UR robot. To find the final solution to our thesis, tests will be performed to determine the best possible subsystems that adheres to the predefined parameters.\\ 

Each subsystem will delve deeper down to each own setting, describing a part of the system, where it is infused into a connected system. Creating a larger system of each part of the project to create the final form resulting in the current technology. \\

The following report describes the thoughts, development and processes that went into the project. Furthermore, it presents the methods applied and the results achieved and discusses the learning outcomes gained throughout the project. \\
